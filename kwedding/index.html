K-Wedding by Honeymoon Resort

K-Wedding is operated by Honeymoon Resort, one of Korea's leading honeymoon and luxury travel agencies. Founded in 1995 and headquartered in Gangnam, Seoul, Honeymoon Resort serves more than 20,000 couples every year.

With 14 regional branches and over 100 sales professionals across Korea and Hawaii, the company runs both tailor-made honeymoon consultation and B2B travel operations, giving K-Wedding a strong foundation in travel planning, partner coordination, and client support.

Over the past three decades, Honeymoon Resort has built close relationships with luxury hotels and resorts worldwide. Its performance record has earned the company exclusive Korea wholesale rights for seven Four Seasons properties, alongside partnerships with Jumeirah, Leading Hotels of the World, Kimpton, Anantara, and Belmond.

★ Named a Brand USA Global Ambassador · July 2026

Honeymoon Resort, Recognized Worldwide

Honeymoon Resort has worked with 13 tourism boards including Hawaii, Dubai, Queensland, and Tahiti to develop honeymoon products and give travelers a wider range of choices. Built on this global network and three decades of honeymoon expertise, the company is recognized as a leading name in Korea's outbound luxury and honeymoon travel sector, most recently through its selection as a Global Ambassador by Brand USA in July 2026.
